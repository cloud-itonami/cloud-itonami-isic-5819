# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-5819 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Production-record logging, production-operation scheduling,
distribution coordination, content-risk-concern escalation, for the
illustrative product line of greeting cards, calendars, and posters
(ISIC 5819 "other publishing activities").

Out-of-scope: Finalizing editorial-content decisions (what a design
actually depicts, whether it ships as designed), or legal-risk
clearance decisions (copyright/trademark/licensed-character clearance).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
